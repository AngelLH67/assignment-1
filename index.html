Featured Event

Welcome to The Local Circuit, a small independent guide run by a volunteer community who spend weekends tracking down local events and activities to share with the public.

Meet the Journalist

Before wrting for The Local Circuit, our journalist shows up at the actual event, talks with volunteers at the water stations, and asks first-timers what nearly tripped them up on the way in. That legwork is what separates this guide from an official event flyer.

This Month's Spotlight

This edition of the guide covers the Peachtree Road Race, one of the largest annual road races in the United States. The full write-up covers what to expect on race morning, a rundown of activities worth planning around, and a set of attendee tips gathered from past participants and spectators.

Why this Guide Exists

Large public events can be intimidating for first-time attendees, and even seasoned participants can miss out on the best parts of an event if they don't know what to look for. The Local Circuit exists to help people get the most out of their local events, whether they're running a race, attending a festival, or just looking for something fun to do on the weekend.

  • Independent reporting, not paid promotion
  • One event covered thoroughly rather than multiple
  • Tips gathered from attendees and volunteers
