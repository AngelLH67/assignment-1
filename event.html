Community Spotlight: Peachtree Road Race

Event Overview

The Peachtree Road Race is an annual 10-kilometer run held in Atlanta, Georgia, on the Fourth of July. It is one of the largest road races in the United States and attracts tens of thousands of participants from around the world. The race is known for its festive atmosphere, with live music, cheering crowds, and a strong sense of community spirit. Participants range from elite athletes to casual runners, making it an inclusive event for all fitness levels. The course finishes near Piedmont Park, where a post-race festival with food, music, and finisher merchandise keeps the celebration going well after the last wave crosses the line.

Featured Activities

  1. Line up in your assigned starting corral based on your qualifying time or wave
  2. Enjoy live bands and DJs stationed along the course to keep runners' energy up
  3. Grab water and sports drink at the aid stations spaced along the route
  4. Cross the finish line and collect your finisher t-shirt near Piedmont Park
  5. Browse the post-race festival for food trucks, sponsor booths, and live music
  6. Check your official time and photos at the results tent after you finish

Attendee Tips

  • Arrive at your corral at least 45 minutes early, since road closures make last-minute access difficult.
  • Use MARTA rail service instead of driving, since most nearby streets close for the race.
  • Wear sunscreen and a hat, since the course offers very little shade during the mid-morning heat.
  • Check the corral assignment on your bib the night before, since it determines where you can enter the start line.
  • Plan to meet spectators well past the finish chute, since the finish area gets crowded and phone signal can be unreliable.

Attendee Testimonial

"I signed up as a walker and still got swept up in the energy of the crowd bands. My favorite part was turning onto the final stretch and hearing total strangers cheering me by name off my bib."

— Marcus T., first-time participant
